## The Theory (The What)

**Overhead** is the additional cost — in memory, CPU time, [allocations]({{ "/en/glossary/allocations/" | relative_url }}), or [bytecode]({{ "/en/glossary/bytecode/" | relative_url }}) size — introduced by an abstraction, pattern, or mechanism beyond what the bare operation requires. Every layer of indirection adds some overhead; the engineering question is whether the benefit justifies the cost.

In Kotlin and Android, common sources of overhead include:

- **[Delegated properties]({{ "/en/01-kotlin-core/delegated-properties/" | relative_url }})**: Each `by` declaration creates a delegate object on the [heap]({{ "/en/glossary/heap/" | relative_url }}), plus a [KProperty]({{ "/en/glossary/kproperty/" | relative_url }}) metadata object per property.
- **[Lambda]({{ "/en/glossary/lambdas/" | relative_url }}) allocations**: Non-inlined lambdas create anonymous class instances. [Inline functions]({{ "/en/glossary/inline-functions/" | relative_url }}) eliminate this overhead.
- **Coroutine machinery**: Each `suspend` call involves a state machine and a `Continuation` object.
- **Reflection**: Accessing `KClass`, annotations, or members at [Runtime]({{ "/en/glossary/runtime/" | relative_url }}) is orders of magnitude slower than direct access.
- **[Autoboxing]({{ "/en/glossary/autoboxing/" | relative_url }})**: Wrapping [primitives]({{ "/en/glossary/primitives/" | relative_url }}) in their object counterparts (`int` → `Integer`).

## The Senior Nuance

- A Senior measures overhead rather than assuming it matters. Profiling tools (Android Studio Profiler, `measureTimeMillis`, benchmark libraries) reveal whether an abstraction's cost is meaningful in context. A `Lazy<T>` delegate's one-time allocation is irrelevant for a screen-level property; the same pattern inside a `RecyclerView.onBindViewHolder` called 1000× per scroll becomes measurable.
- Kotlin's compiler eliminates many overhead sources: `inline` erases lambda allocation, `value class` erases wrapper allocation, `const val` erases field lookup. A Senior knows which language features have zero-cost abstractions and which don't.
- The phrase "zero overhead" is aspirational, not literal. Even [inline functions]({{ "/en/glossary/inline-functions/" | relative_url }}) can increase [bytecode]({{ "/en/glossary/bytecode/" | relative_url }}) size ([code bloat]({{ "/en/glossary/code-bloat/" | relative_url }})), trading allocation overhead for method-size overhead.
