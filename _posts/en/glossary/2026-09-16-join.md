## The Theory (The What)

**`Job.join()`** is the [suspend function]({{ "/en/glossary/suspend-functions/" | relative_url }}) that waits for a [`Job`]({{ "/en/glossary/job/" | relative_url }}) to complete — normally, by failure, or by cancellation — and returns `Unit`. It is the "wait" half of [`launch`]({{ "/en/glossary/launch/" | relative_url }}): you get a `Job`, and `join()` is how you suspend until it is done. Unlike [`await()`]({{ "/en/glossary/await/" | relative_url }}), it does not rethrow the job's exception; a failed job simply *completes*, and `join()` returns.

```kotlin
// Not found in FAS — standalone example
val sync = scope.launch { repository.sync() }
sync.join()                 // suspend until sync finishes, whatever the outcome
showSyncFinishedToast()
```

## The Senior Nuance

- **You rarely need it under structured concurrency.** [`coroutineScope`]({{ "/en/glossary/coroutine-scope-builder/" | relative_url }}) already joins every child. Explicit `join()` appears when the `Job` was launched on a *different* scope than the one waiting.
- **`join()` swallows nothing, but reports nothing either.** The failure still propagates through the tree to the parent; `join()` just does not surface it to the caller. If you need the outcome, it should have been [`async`]({{ "/en/glossary/async/" | relative_url }}).
- **`joinAll(vararg jobs)` and `cancelAndJoin()` are the two useful variants.** The second is the correct way to stop a job *and* know it has stopped before continuing.
- See [Launch vs Async/Await]({{ "/en/02-coroutines-flow/launch-vs-async-await/" | relative_url }}).
