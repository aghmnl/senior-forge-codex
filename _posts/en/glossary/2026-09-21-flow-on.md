## The Theory (The What)

**`flowOn(context)`** is the [`Flow`]({{ "/en/glossary/flow/" | relative_url }}) operator that changes the [coroutine context]({{ "/en/glossary/coroutine-context/" | relative_url }}) — in practice, the [dispatcher]({{ "/en/glossary/dispatcher/" | relative_url }}) — of everything [upstream]({{ "/en/glossary/upstream/" | relative_url }}) of it, leaving the [downstream]({{ "/en/glossary/downstream/" | relative_url }}) operators and the [`collect`]({{ "/en/glossary/collect/" | relative_url }}) block in the collector's own context. It is the stream counterpart of [`withContext`]({{ "/en/glossary/with-context/" | relative_url }}), and the only sanctioned way to cross a dispatcher boundary inside a flow, because [context preservation]({{ "/en/glossary/context-preservation/" | relative_url }}) forbids emitting from another context.

```kotlin
// Not found in FAS — standalone example
fun importedFiles(dir: File): Flow<Backup> = flow {
    dir.listFiles().orEmpty().forEach { emit(parseBackup(it.readText())) }
}.flowOn(ioDispatcher)     // producer + parsing run on IO; collector unaffected
```

## The Senior Nuance

- **Position is the API.** `flowOn` only affects what is written above it, so it goes immediately after the blocking producer. At the end of a chain in a ViewModel it moves the *whole* pipeline off Main, including mapping meant to feed UI state.
- **It implies a [buffer]({{ "/en/glossary/buffer/" | relative_url }}).** Crossing dispatchers needs a channel, so `flowOn` also decouples producer from collector — faster pipelines, less predictable emission timing in tests.
- **Do not stack it on already-safe sources.** [Room]({{ "/en/glossary/room/" | relative_url }}), [DataStore]({{ "/en/glossary/datastore/" | relative_url }}) and [Retrofit]({{ "/en/glossary/retrofit/" | relative_url }}) flows already emit off Main; adding `flowOn` costs a hop and misinforms the next reader.
- **`flowOn(Dispatchers.Main)` pins the *producer* to Main**, not the collector — almost never what is intended.
- See [withContext vs flowOn]({{ "/en/02-coroutines-flow/with-context-vs-flow-on/" | relative_url }}).
