## The Theory (The What)

**FollowApp Suite (FAS)** is the Android project, a suite of productivity apps built with [Kotlin]({{ "/en/glossary/kotlin/" | relative_url }}), [Jetpack Compose]({{ "/en/glossary/jetpack-compose/" | relative_url }}), [coroutines]({{ "/en/glossary/coroutines/" | relative_url }}), [Flow]({{ "/en/glossary/flow/" | relative_url }}) and [Hilt]({{ "/en/glossary/hilt/" | relative_url }}), that this Codex uses as its source of real code. Whenever an article's **Code in Action** section can show a concept with code that runs in production, the snippet comes from FAS and is marked with a `// From FollowApp Suite — FileName.kt` comment. When a concept is not used there, the example is marked `// Not found in FAS — standalone example`.

## The Senior Nuance

- **Why real code.** Interview answers are stronger when they come from decisions made in a real codebase, with its trade-offs, than from textbook snippets.
- **FAS only appears in Code in Action.** The theory and the senior perspective of each article are written independently of it, so they apply to any project.
