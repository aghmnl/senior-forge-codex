## The Theory (The What)

**Skipping** is how Compose avoids rerunning a composable during [recomposition]({{ "/en/glossary/recomposition/" | relative_url }}). When a parent recomposes and calls a child again, Compose compares the new arguments with the previous ones; if all are equal, the child's body does not run and its previous output is reused. [Stable]({{ "/en/glossary/stability/" | relative_url }}) parameters are compared with [`equals`]({{ "/en/glossary/equals/" | relative_url }}); with [strong skipping]({{ "/en/glossary/strong-skipping/" | relative_url }}), [unstable]({{ "/en/glossary/stability/" | relative_url }}) ones are compared by instance ([`===`]({{ "/en/glossary/referential-equality/" | relative_url }})).

## The Senior Nuance

- **Skipping depends on what you pass.** A new instance on every call (a [list]({{ "/en/glossary/list/" | relative_url }}) built with [`filter`]({{ "/en/glossary/filter/" | relative_url }}) in the parent's body) defeats it, even if the content is the same.
- **Measure it, do not guess.** The [Layout Inspector]({{ "/en/glossary/layout-inspector/" | relative_url }}) shows how many times each composable was recomposed and skipped.
