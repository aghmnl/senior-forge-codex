## The Theory (The What)

An **Activity** is the Android component that represents a screen the user interacts with: it owns a window and goes through a lifecycle (`onCreate`, `onStart`, `onResume`, `onPause`, `onStop`, `onDestroy`) as the user and the system move it to the foreground and background. In a Compose app there is usually a single Activity that calls `setContent { }`, and navigation happens between composables inside it.

## The Senior Nuance

- **The system can destroy and recreate it** on a [configuration change]({{ "/en/glossary/configuration-change/" | relative_url }}) or after [process death]({{ "/en/glossary/process-death/" | relative_url }}), so it must never be the only place where state lives.
- **Do not hold references to it from longer-lived objects** ([singletons]({{ "/en/glossary/singleton/" | relative_url }}), ViewModels): that leaks the whole screen.
