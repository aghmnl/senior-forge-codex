## The Theory (The What)

A **`Bundle`** is Android's key-value container for passing small data between components and across processes: Intent extras, Fragment arguments and the [saved instance state]({{ "/en/glossary/saved-instance-state/" | relative_url }}) are all Bundles. It holds [primitives]({{ "/en/glossary/primitives/" | relative_url }}), [`String`]({{ "/en/glossary/string/" | relative_url }}), arrays of them, [`Parcelable`]({{ "/en/glossary/parcelable/" | relative_url }}) and [`Serializable`]({{ "/en/glossary/serializable/" | relative_url }}) values, and other Bundles.

```kotlin
// Not found in FAS — standalone example
val args = bundleOf("taskId" to task.id, "readOnly" to true)
```

## The Senior Nuance

- **It crosses process boundaries through Binder**, which is why it has a size limit and why a large Bundle fails with a [`TransactionTooLargeException`]({{ "/en/glossary/transaction-too-large-exception/" | relative_url }}).
- **Put ids in it, not objects.** The receiver loads the data from its real source.
