## The Theory (The What)

**`Box`** **stacks** its children on top of each other, in order. `contentAlignment` positions all of them, and `Modifier.align(Alignment)` (from [`BoxScope`]({{ "/en/glossary/layout-scopes/" | relative_url }})) positions one, in both axes.

```kotlin
// Not found in FAS — standalone example
Box(contentAlignment = Alignment.Center) { Image(...); Text("Overlay", Modifier.align(Alignment.BottomEnd)) }
```

## The Senior Nuance

- **The tool for overlays**: badges, a chip on a card border, a loader on top of content.
- See [Modifiers: Order & Alignment]({{ "/en/03-jetpack-compose/modifiers-order-alignment/" | relative_url }}).
