## The Theory (The What)

**`Modifier.clip(shape)`** clips everything that comes **after it** in the chain to a shape (`CircleShape`, `RoundedCornerShape(12.dp)`): backgrounds, content and the [ripple]({{ "/en/glossary/ripple/" | relative_url }}) of a later [`clickable`]({{ "/en/glossary/clickable/" | relative_url }}). [Modifiers]({{ "/en/glossary/modifier/" | relative_url }}) placed before it are not clipped.

```kotlin
// Not found in FAS — standalone example
Box(Modifier.size(40.dp).clip(CircleShape).background(Color.Gray).clickable { })
```

## The Senior Nuance

- **`clip` then `background` is a round background; `background` then `clip` is a square one**, because the background was drawn before clipping.
- See [Modifiers: Order & Alignment]({{ "/en/03-jetpack-compose/modifiers-order-alignment/" | relative_url }}).
