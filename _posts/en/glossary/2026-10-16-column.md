## The Theory (The What)

**`Column`** places its children **vertically**. `verticalArrangement` distributes them along that [main axis]({{ "/en/glossary/main-axis-cross-axis/" | relative_url }}), and `horizontalAlignment` positions them on the horizontal [cross axis]({{ "/en/glossary/main-axis-cross-axis/" | relative_url }}). [`ColumnScope`]({{ "/en/glossary/layout-scopes/" | relative_url }}) provides [`weight`]({{ "/en/glossary/weight/" | relative_url }}) and `align(Alignment.Horizontal)`.

```kotlin
// Not found in FAS — standalone example
Column(verticalArrangement = Arrangement.spacedBy(12.dp), horizontalAlignment = Alignment.CenterHorizontally) { /* ... */ }
```

## The Senior Nuance

- **It composes every child**, visible or not; for long lists use [`LazyColumn`]({{ "/en/glossary/lazy-column/" | relative_url }}).
- See [Modifiers: Order & Alignment]({{ "/en/03-jetpack-compose/modifiers-order-alignment/" | relative_url }}).
