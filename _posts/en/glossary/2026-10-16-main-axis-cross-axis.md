## The Theory (The What)

In a [`Row`]({{ "/en/glossary/row/" | relative_url }}) or [`Column`]({{ "/en/glossary/column/" | relative_url }}), the **main axis** is the direction children are placed in (horizontal for [`Row`]({{ "/en/glossary/row/" | relative_url }}), vertical for [`Column`]({{ "/en/glossary/column/" | relative_url }})), and the **cross axis** is the perpendicular one. [Arrangement]({{ "/en/glossary/arrangement/" | relative_url }}) works on the main axis; [alignment]({{ "/en/glossary/alignment/" | relative_url }}) on the cross axis.

## The Senior Nuance

- **Naming the axis removes the confusion**: "horizontal" means [arrangement]({{ "/en/glossary/arrangement/" | relative_url }}) in a [`Row`]({{ "/en/glossary/row/" | relative_url }}) but [alignment]({{ "/en/glossary/alignment/" | relative_url }}) in a [`Column`]({{ "/en/glossary/column/" | relative_url }}).
