## The Theory (The What)

A **`Modifier`** is an ordered chain of decorations applied to a composable: size, padding, background, clipping, click handling, positioning. Each modifier wraps everything that comes after it, from the outside in, so `Modifier.padding(8.dp).background(Color.Red)` and `Modifier.background(Color.Red).padding(8.dp)` look different.

```kotlin
// Not found in FAS — standalone example
Text("Hi", Modifier.padding(16.dp).background(Color.Red))
```

## The Senior Nuance

- **Order is the whole point.** Read the chain as layers, outermost first.
- **Every UI composable should accept `modifier: Modifier = Modifier`** and apply it first, once, at its root.
