## The Theory (The What)

**`Modifier.padding(...)`** adds space around a composable's content. Where it sits in the chain decides what that space is: **before** a [`background`]({{ "/en/glossary/background/" | relative_url }}) or a [`clickable`]({{ "/en/glossary/clickable/" | relative_url }}), it is outside them and works like a margin; **after** them, it is inside them, between their edge and the content.

```kotlin
// Not found in FAS — standalone example
Text("A", Modifier.padding(16.dp).background(Color.Red))   // margin, then red
Text("B", Modifier.background(Color.Red).padding(16.dp))   // red, with padding inside
```

## The Senior Nuance

- **Compose has no margin [modifier]({{ "/en/glossary/modifier/" | relative_url }})**: a margin is just padding placed before the [modifiers]({{ "/en/glossary/modifier/" | relative_url }}) it should stay outside of.
- **Prefer `Arrangement.spacedBy` to padding on every child** when the space is between siblings.
