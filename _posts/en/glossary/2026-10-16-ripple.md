## The Theory (The What)

The **ripple** is Material's press indication: the circular wave drawn over an element when it is tapped. [`clickable`]({{ "/en/glossary/clickable/" | relative_url }}) shows it by default, and it is clipped by any [`clip`]({{ "/en/glossary/clip/" | relative_url }}) placed before [`clickable`]({{ "/en/glossary/clickable/" | relative_url }}) in the chain.

## The Senior Nuance

- **It is feedback, not decoration**: without it, users cannot tell a tap registered.
- **A square ripple on a round button is an ordering bug**: [`clip`]({{ "/en/glossary/clip/" | relative_url }}) is missing or after [`clickable`]({{ "/en/glossary/clickable/" | relative_url }}).
