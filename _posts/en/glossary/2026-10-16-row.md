## The Theory (The What)

**`Row`** places its children **horizontally**, one after another. `horizontalArrangement` distributes them along that [main axis]({{ "/en/glossary/main-axis-cross-axis/" | relative_url }}), and `verticalAlignment` positions them on the vertical [cross axis]({{ "/en/glossary/main-axis-cross-axis/" | relative_url }}). Inside it, `Modifier.weight` and `Modifier.align(Alignment.Vertical)` are available through [`RowScope`]({{ "/en/glossary/layout-scopes/" | relative_url }}).

```kotlin
// Not found in FAS — standalone example
Row(horizontalArrangement = Arrangement.spacedBy(8.dp), verticalAlignment = Alignment.CenterVertically) { Icon(...); Text(...) }
```

## The Senior Nuance

- **It does not scroll**; for many items use `LazyRow`.
- See [Modifiers: Order & Alignment]({{ "/en/03-jetpack-compose/modifiers-order-alignment/" | relative_url }}).
