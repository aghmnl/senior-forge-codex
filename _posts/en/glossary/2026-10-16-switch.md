## The Theory (The What)

**`Switch(checked, onCheckedChange)`** is Material's on/off toggle composable. It follows the same [stateless]({{ "/en/glossary/stateful-stateless/" | relative_url }}) contract as [`Checkbox`]({{ "/en/glossary/checkbox/" | relative_url }}): it shows `checked` and reports the user's toggle through `onCheckedChange`; the caller stores the value.

```kotlin
// Not found in FAS — standalone example
Switch(checked = settings.darkMode, onCheckedChange = onDarkModeChange)
```

## The Senior Nuance

- **Use it for settings that apply immediately**; a [`Checkbox`]({{ "/en/glossary/checkbox/" | relative_url }}) fits selections that are confirmed later, such as items in a form.
- See [State Hoisting]({{ "/en/03-jetpack-compose/state-hoisting/" | relative_url }}).
