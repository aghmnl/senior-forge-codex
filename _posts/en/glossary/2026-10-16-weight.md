## The Theory (The What)

**`Modifier.weight(1f)`**, available in [`RowScope`]({{ "/en/glossary/layout-scopes/" | relative_url }}) and [`ColumnScope`]({{ "/en/glossary/layout-scopes/" | relative_url }}), gives a child **the space left** after the unweighted children are measured, split among the weighted ones in proportion to their weights.

```kotlin
// Not found in FAS — standalone example
Row { Text(title, Modifier.weight(1f), maxLines = 1); Icon(Icons.Default.ChevronRight, null) }
```

## The Senior Nuance

- **The clean way to push an element to the end**: the weighted text takes the rest and the icon keeps its size.
- **With `fill = false`** the child can be smaller than its share.
- See [Modifiers: Order & Alignment]({{ "/en/03-jetpack-compose/modifiers-order-alignment/" | relative_url }}).
